What we believe

A new way of seeing children.

Children growing up in the 21st century need to be more human than any generation before them. They need to think, adapt and solve real problems with their hearts, hands and imaginations.

  1. 01

    Children are capable and can lead

    Every child holds a treasure trove of creativity. Play, exploration, real-world experiences, failures and successes all bring it to life. When children own their learning, they own the results.

  2. 02

    Learning is fastest when the whole child is involved

    The brain learns best by doing: hands busy, senses awake, heart engaged.

  3. 03

    Real life is the curriculum

    Children learn to fix what breaks at home, adapt when plans fall apart, and pivot when things go awry.

  4. 04

    Local collaboration is our teacher and our guide

    We work hand in hand with local businesses and institutions of excellence. Their people, their workplaces and their wisdom enrich everything our children learn.

  5. 05

    Children can contribute now, at any age

    To their families, their neighborhoods and the world.

  6. 06

    Our word matters

    Children learn to make promises and keep them, to do their work well, to treat people fairly and to be trustworthy. Every week they set goals, pledge them to a partner and follow through.

  7. 07

    Failure is a teacher

    We don’t shield children from it; we help them learn from it and try again.

  8. 08

    Friendships matter

    Children’s friendships are not trivial, and we nurture them. Friendship is where peace begins.

  9. 09

    There is strength in diversity of thought and expression

    Loud or quiet, talkative or reflective, energetic or calm, each child’s nature is an asset to society, and that is reflected in how every child is treated at EP.

  10. 10

    Civic responsibility is a core tenet

    Children learn to speak up, listen and serve the common good, in communication coaching, children’s Town Hall meetings, Model UN, our podcast and daily reflection.

  11. 11

    We are a global village

    Children grow into good neighbors at home and good citizens of their communities and the world, with empathy for the whole human family.

  12. 12

    School can spark joy

    Learning, even when it gets hard or dull, can bring flow, creativity and anticipation when it’s paired with exploration, purpose and real-world immersion.

Why flow?

Flow is that feeling of being so absorbed in something you love that time disappears. Psychologist Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi named it.

We spark it in childhood, so kids seek it for life.

How we build it

  • Kids learn through real-life projects.
  • Failure and success are both welcome teachers.
  • We help kids find what unleashes their imagination and joy.
  • Play sits at the center of work, even hard work.

Ingredients baked into our day: Learn→Explore→Aspire→Play

Entrepreneurs at Play

An academic wing of FamilyCoachMD

Dr. Larisa Che founded FamilyCoachMD to support children and the adults who care for them through psychiatry, family coaching, parenting courses and school consulting.

Entrepreneurs at Play is its education program. Through enrichment, summer camp and the microschool, children build confidence, practice working with others and learn by doing.
