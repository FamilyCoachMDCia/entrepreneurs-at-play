Children growing up in the 21st century need to be more human than any generation before them. They need to think, adapt and solve real problems with their hearts, hands and imaginations.

What we grow in every child

Why flow?

Flow is that feeling of being so absorbed in something you love that time disappears. Psychologist Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi named it.

We spark it in childhood, so kids seek it for life.

How we build it

  • Kids learn through real-life projects.
  • Failure and success are both welcome teachers.
  • We help kids find what unleashes their imagination and joy.
  • Play sits at the center of work, even hard work.

Entrepreneurs at Play

An academic wing of FamilyCoachMD

Dr. Larisa Che founded FamilyCoachMD to support children and the adults who care for them through psychiatry, family coaching, parenting courses and school consulting.

Entrepreneurs at Play is its education program. Through enrichment, summer camp and the microschool, children build confidence, practice working with others and learn by doing.
