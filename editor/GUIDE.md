# How to edit the Entrepreneurs at Play website

A short guide for Dr. Che. You change words, photos and colors in a simple editor.
When you press **Save**, the change goes live on the website in about 1 to 2 minutes.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the editor: **[Open the EP editor](https://entrepreneurs-at-play-editor.vercel.app/keystatic)**
2. Click **Sign in** and use the email you created your Keystatic account with.
3. On the left you will see the pages: Home page, Enrichment, Summer, Microschool,
   Who we are, and "Whole site" and "Colors".

## 2. Change a sentence

1. Click the page on the left (for example **Home page**).
2. Find the box with the sentence. The small gray note under each box tells you what it
   changes and how long it can be (for example "max 70 characters").
3. Click in the box and type.
4. Click **Save** at the top. Wait 1 to 2 minutes and refresh the website.

If you type too much, the box turns red and Save will not work. Shorten the sentence
and try again. That is on purpose: it keeps the pages looking right.

Tip: "Whole site" holds the tagline ("The world is our canvas, Play is our guide."),
the announcement bar at the top of every page, and the footer.

## 3. Swap a photo

1. Open the page and find the photo (for example **Golf block, Photo**).
2. Choose **Upload a new photo**, then click to pick a picture from your computer.
   Or choose **A photo from the library** to use one that is already on the site.
3. Best shape: a **landscape** photo, wider than tall (3:2, like a regular camera photo),
   at least 1200 pixels wide.
4. Fill in **Photo description**. Write one simple sentence about what the picture shows,
   for example "Three children building a robot at a table". It helps people who use
   screen readers, and it is required.
5. Click **Save**.

The website makes the photo smaller and removes hidden camera and location data for you.

## 4. Change a door color or the background

1. Click **Colors** on the left.
2. Under **Program colors**, pick a color for Enrichment, Summer and Microschool.
   The choices are Sand, Sun, Tan, Clay blush, Sage and Mist. They are chosen so the
   writing always stays easy to read.
3. Under **Page background**, pick Cream (today's look), Linen or Milk.
   The notebook grid always stays.
4. Click **Save**.

## 5. Mark something "Coming soon"

Days of the week, The Hatch and some sessions have a **Coming soon** box.
Tick it to show the "Coming soon" tag. Untick it when it is confirmed.

## Please remember

- **The website is public, and so is its history.** Never type phone numbers, home
  addresses, or children's names or faces without permission from the family.
  Anything you save can be seen by anyone.
- Every Save goes live. There is no draft, so read it once before you click Save.
- Some things (layout, animations, the contact form, new sections) are done by Ithalo.

## If something looks wrong

Do not worry. **Message Ithalo.** Every change can be undone in a few seconds, and
nothing you do in the editor can break the website for good.
