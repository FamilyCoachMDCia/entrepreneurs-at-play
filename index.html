Entrepreneurs at Play is its education program. Through enrichment, summer camp and the microschool, children build confidence, practice working with others and learn by doing.

Quick answers

Questions parents ask

What is EP?

What is EP?

Entrepreneurs at Play is an Omaha enrichment center founded by Dr. Larisa Che. It offers after-school enrichment, summer camp and a mixed-age microschool.

Who can join after-school enrichment?

Children from local schools and homeschool families can join. T6 Golf & Finance is for ages 8–12. Ask about ages and schedules for the other activities.

Can I enroll in the microschool?

Spring beta enrollment is at capacity. You can request a place on the Fall 2027 waitlist.

Where?

EP is based in the Omaha area. Ask us for the location of the program you’re interested in.

How much does it cost?

Fees vary by program. Email us for current fees.

How is the microschool staffed?

Two adults are on site at all times, background-checked and trained in CPR, first aid and child safety.

What are The Hatch and The Studio?

Our two pathways. The Hatch is for ages 2 to 6, and The Studio is for ages 7 to 12. They share one community, and through Axial Mentorship, Studio kids mentor Hatch kids, much like a close family. Each child works at their own level.

How does the microschool track progress?

Academics follow Nebraska standards, and NWEA MAP Growth testing twice a year tracks each child’s progress.

How much screen time?

The microschool uses screens sparingly. Older children may use technology for specific projects, such as recording and producing a podcast.

What about children who learn differently?

Our founder is a board-certified child and adolescent psychiatrist, and our small size lets us adapt to each child. Contact us, and we will be honest about whether EP is the right fit.

Is the program religious?

EP welcomes families of all faiths and cultures. Reflection drawn from many traditions is one part of our arts and spirituality strand.
