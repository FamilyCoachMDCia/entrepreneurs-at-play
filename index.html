Quick answers

Questions parents ask

Is this a school?

Is this a school?

Yes. We run 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., five days a week, and operate like a microschool; our school license is pending. Homeschooled children and families seeking school-break enrichment are welcome.

Where?

In the Omaha area. Our location will be announced soon.

How much does it cost?

Email us for program fee details.

Is it safe?

Two adults are on site at all times, background-checked and trained in CPR, first aid and child safety.

What are The Hatch and The Studio?

Our two pathways. The Hatch is for ages 2 to 6, and The Studio is for ages 7 to 12. They share one community, and through Axial Mentorship, Studio kids mentor Hatch kids, much like a close family. Each child works at their own level.

Will my child keep up academically?

Academics follow Nebraska standards, and NWEA MAP Growth testing twice a year tracks each child’s progress.

How much screen time?

Very little. Technology is limited and purposeful, and our youngest have almost none.

What about children who learn differently?

Our founder is a board-certified child and adolescent psychiatrist, and our small size lets us adapt to each child. Contact us, and we will be honest about whether EP is the right fit.

Is the program religious?

EP welcomes families of all faiths and cultures. Reflection drawn from many traditions is one part of our arts and spirituality strand.
